DOCUMENTATION 

Documentation Mode

Here you label and structure your network and export it as an image or report.

Tools in the bar

Text
Explanations and labels. Drag it from the bar onto the workspace; a text field is moved by the bar above its text, and you format the text with the context menu.

Structure Field
A field that groups several devices visually. Drag it from the bar and change its size at the edge; new fields are white, you change the colour with the context menu. The small + at its top left adds a label that belongs to the field and moves with it, e.g. the name of the network.

Export
Saves the picture of the network as an image file.

Report
Creates a PDF file with a picture of the network and the most important settings of the devices.

Tip: In the learning path, every network gets a structure field around all its devices. At the top left of the field there is the label with name and network address, e.g. "School network 192.168.10.0/24" – quickest with the + of the field, a text field works too. Routers stay between the fields.
