DESIGN 

Design Mode

Here you build your network and configure the devices.

Adding devices

Drag a component with the mouse from the bar on the left onto the workspace.

Dragging a PC onto the workspace

Delete a device with its context menu (right mouse button on the device) or with the  Del  key while it is selected.

Context menu of a device

Connecting devices

Select Connection in the bar (click or  Alt + 1 ) and then click the two devices one after the other.

The connection follows the mouse to the second device.

Remove a connection with the context menu of a connected device (Remove cable).

Wireless works too: set the PC or laptop to Wireless (Wi-Fi) and choose the network name (SSID) of a Switch / Wi-Fi.

Configuring devices

Double-click a device to open its settings at the bottom. You can choose any name; it does not change the network. All other settings decide how data flows.

Settings of a PC

PC and Laptop
Need an IPv4 address and a netmask, and a gateway to reach other networks. They can also get their settings by DHCP if a DHCP server is running in the network. In the learning path, laptops are the devices people work and browse on; PCs offer something to others as servers, e.g. web pages.

Switch / Wi-Fi
Connects devices in the same network and needs no settings. With a network name (SSID) it is also a Wi-Fi access point. Display as Cloud shows it as a cloud, for example for the internet.

Home Router
Connects a home network (LAN) to the internet (WAN), like the router at home. It translates addresses (NAT), can hand out addresses by DHCP and offers a firewall and port forwarding.

Router
Connects different networks. Each network card gets an address from its network. The forwarding table decides where packets go: set it by hand or with Automatic Routing. A firewall can be set up here or in simulation mode.

Tip: Devices in another network can only be reached if the gateway is set to the address of the router in your own network.

Learning path

The Start learning path button next to the modes opens the learning path. The first time, you choose between Standard (8 chapters) and Advanced (12 chapters, with HTTPS, home network, Wi-Fi, port forwarding, firewall and VPN). Every chapter has reading, practice, quiz and glossary. Filius checks the practice tasks in the running network and ticks them off as soon as they are really done. The ring shows how many chapters you have finished. The progress of both learning paths is saved with your project; when you open the project again, it continues at the same place. In the overview you can switch the learning path.

New, Open, Save

New starts an empty project, and the learning path starts from the beginning. Open opens a saved project (.fls), Save saves your network together with your progress in the learning path. Filius does not keep anything by itself: it always starts empty, and what is not in your .fls file is lost when Filius closes. So Filius asks first when you close it with unsaved changes.

Language

Filius is available in English and German, including the learning path. Click the DE | EN switch at the top right to switch to German: Filius asks first, offers to save unsaved work and restarts.

Light and dark

To the right of DE | EN is the switch with sun and moon. A click toggles between the light and the dark look, without a restart. Filius remembers your choice for the next start. The help and the pictures in the learning path and in the help follow your choice.
